Design System

Inventory-mode gallery for the Sebastian & Flemy wedding invitation (single-file static HTML, no build step). Every specimen below links the project's real site-styles.css (a verbatim copy of index.html's inline <style> block) and reuses real markup/classes — never hand-faked CSS.

Theme: single light theme only — no dark mode or alt-theme block exists in the source, so no theme toggle is built here (per the skill's theme-detection rule: 0/1 theme = no toggle).

Specimens tagged posed render a static snapshot of state that production drives with JavaScript (scroll-reveal, countdown, family-card expand) — this gallery does not load the site's own <script>, so nothing here is scroll- or timer-driven. Specimens tagged dead css mark classes/tokens that exist in the stylesheet but never render on the live page.

Foundations · Layout

A single vertical column of full-bleed sections, each with a centered inner content wrapper. Content column caps at 720px by default; denser blocks narrow further — RSVP card 480px, ceremony timeline 580px, faith section 600px.

Card layouts use CSS Grid (one column on phones, two above 560px); nav, hero, dividers, and card internals use flex. The hero is a viewport-height grid (1fr auto) pinning the countdown to the bottom edge. Global: overflow-x clipped, border-box reset, scroll-padding-top:72px so anchor jumps clear the fixed nav.

Domain · Ceremony Timeline

.cer-sec / .tl live [inline]
Syro-Malabar Catholic

The Ceremony

The Syro-Malabar Church traces its roots to St. Thomas the Apostle, who arrived in Kerala in 52 AD. The wedding is celebrated as a sacred sacrament within the Holy Qurbana — the ancient East Syriac liturgy.

Madhuramveppu

A sweet-offering ceremony where rice cooked in milk and jaggery is shared as a blessing, symbolising a life filled with sweetness and grace.

Nischayam — The Betrothal

A formal church blessing where the couple exchange rings before the priest, publicly declaring their intent to marry as a sacred covenant.

The Rite of Marriage

The couple declare their intent, exchange solemn vows, and the priest blesses the rings — a binding covenant before God and all who witness.

Minnu & Manthrakodi

The groom ties the Minnu — a sacred gold pendant — around the bride's neck, then drapes the silk Manthrakodi kasavu saree over her shoulders.

Nuptial Qurbana & Blessing

The couple receive the Nuptial Blessing and participate in the Holy Eucharist together for the first time as husband and wife.

Signing & Recessional

The couple sign the register and walk out as husband and wife while the choir fills the church with song and joy.
